About Me

Business-minded.
Hands-on by nature.

I'm currently pursuing an Associate in Arts at Wake Technical Community College and preparing to transfer into a four-year business program, with a focus on marketing.

My interest in marketing comes from the mix of strategy, psychology, creativity, and technology behind how organizations connect with people. I enjoy looking beyond what a business sells to understand how it communicates, earns trust, and creates a useful experience for customers.

Outside class, I've put that interest into practice by building websites, helping shape brands, researching marketing questions, and running day-to-day operations for a small service business. I like learning by doing, solving problems as they come up, and improving a project through feedback.
