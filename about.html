THE STORY SO FAR

A Little Background

Curious by nature.
Competitive by choice.

I'm a student at Wake Technical Community College working toward an Associate in Arts, with plans to study business administration and marketing after transferring to a four-year university.

I've been interested in the strategy behind business for years—especially how brands communicate, how people make decisions, and how creativity and technology can help an idea reach the right audience. That interest has grown beyond the classroom through marketing research, website development, and helping build small businesses.

Outside of school and work, soccer has been a constant. I captained my high school varsity team for four years and have learned that leadership is not just about speaking up. It is about showing up consistently, helping teammates improve, and staying composed when things do not go as planned.
